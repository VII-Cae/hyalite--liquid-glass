A page with things behind the island

Click the record in the corner. The island grows into a card; when it stops moving, the lens map is built for its final size and the refraction ramps in — Apple calls that materialize.

Colour blocks and text give the lens something to bend. Drag this page under the island by scrolling.

Hyalite only bends what is behind the glass. The tint layer and the record sit above the refracted backdrop, so a solid or semi-transparent fill stays exactly as you painted it.

Two states, one element: width, height and border-radius transition together; the glass appears only in card state.
